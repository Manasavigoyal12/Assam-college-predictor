Documents checklist (BCECEB)
Keep originals + self-attested copies ready for verification
  • NEET 2026 Admit Card & Scorecard / Rank Card
  • Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Certificate (PCB marks)
  • Bihar Domicile / Residential Certificate (issued by SDO/CO)
  • Caste Certificate — BC-I, BC-II, SC or ST (issued by competent authority, Bihar format)
  • EWS Income & Asset Certificate — if claiming EWS
  • Migration / Transfer Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card & 10+ passport photos (white background)
  • Gap Certificate (if applicable)
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Fill every available option — PMCH, NMCH, Darbhanga, Bhagalpur first, then remaining govt, then private govt-quota
  • Government colleges have the same fee band — order purely by preference, not fee
  • Private college state-quota seats cost ~₹6.2L/yr — much less than management quota (₹10–18L/yr)
  • Reserved-category candidates also compete for UR seats — add UR options after all reserved choices
  • Never miss the reporting deadline after allotment — forfeiture ends your counselling journey
  • If allotted a lower-preference seat, stay in the Round 2 upgrade pool
