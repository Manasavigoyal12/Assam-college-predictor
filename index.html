Documents checklist (RAJUGNEET)
Keep originals + self-attested copies ready for verification
  • NEET 2026 Admit Card & Rank Card / Scorecard
  • Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Certificate (PCB marks)
  • Rajasthan Domicile / Bonafide Certificate
  • Caste Certificate — OBC / MBC / SC / ST (Rajasthan format, competent authority)
  • EWS Income & Asset Certificate (if claiming EWS) — valid for current year
  • Transfer / Migration Certificate from last institution
  • Character Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card & 10+ passport photos (white background)
  • PWD / NCC / Sports certificate — if claiming sub-category
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Fill SMS Jaipur, RUHS, SN Jodhpur, SP Bikaner, RNT Udaipur first — these close earliest
  • All 33 government colleges charge the same tuition — order by preference only
  • Private general-quota seats (allotted by RAJUGNEET) cost far less than management quota
  • MBC sub-category closes at much lower ranks than OBC — claim it if eligible
  • Home district and local sub-categories can significantly lower your effective cutoff
  • Never miss the security-deposit window — missing it forfeits your allotment
  • Understand the Upgrade / Freeze options before Round 2 — wrong choice ends your journey
