Documents checklist (DME MP)
Keep originals + self-attested copies ready for verification
  • NEET 2026 Admit Card & Rank Card / Scorecard
  • Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Certificate (PCB marks)
  • Madhya Pradesh Domicile (Mulnivasi) Certificate
  • Caste Certificate — OBC-NCL / SC / ST (Madhya Pradesh format, competent authority)
  • EWS Income & Asset Certificate (if claiming EWS) — valid for current year
  • Transfer / Migration Certificate from last institution
  • Character Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card & 10+ passport photos (white background)
  • PWD / NCC / Sports certificate — if claiming sub-category
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Fill MGM Indore, Gandhi Bhopal, NSCB Jabalpur, Gajra Raja Gwalior first — these close earliest
  • All MP government colleges charge the same state tuition — order them by preference only
  • Government seats (~₹1L/yr) are the most affordable path — always list every reachable one first
  • Private state-quota seats (allotted by DME MP) are cheaper than NRI/management quota
  • Deemed universities are separate (MCC counselling) — no MP domicile needed, but fees are higher
  • Never miss the security-deposit / reporting window — missing it forfeits your allotment
  • Understand the Upgrade / Freeze options before Round 2 — a wrong choice can end your journey
